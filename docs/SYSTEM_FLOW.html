1. How the System Flows — Module Overview

Every process in GCGEA MLBMS starts with a member record and ends in a posted, released, or paid transaction that shows up in Reports. Administration governs who can do what, and who approves what, across all of it.

Dashed gold line: Annual Budgets and Disbursements under Financial Management also pass through the same Approval Workflow before they're Approved or Paid.

2. The Approval Workflow (Applies to Loans, Benefits, Member Registration, Budgets & Disbursements)

Most transactions that move money or change a member's status pass through the same four-role pattern before they take effect. The specific people or roles standing in each lane are configured once, per module, in Administration → Approval Workflow.

3. Member Registration Flow

A member only becomes transactable — able to contribute, borrow, or claim benefits — once their registration clears this flow.

4. Loan Application & Repayment Flow

The longest-running flow in the system: from application, through eligibility and approval, to monthly repayment, and — if the member qualifies again — a reloan.

After release, a loan cycles between Loan Payments and Overdue (missed due date → penalty applied on the next payment → back to Loan Payments) until the balance reaches zero and it becomes Fully Paid, at which point the member may Reloan.

5. Benefit Claim Flow

Benefit claims (retirement, mortuary assistance, etc.) follow the same review/approve/release pattern, gated by an automatic eligibility check specific to each benefit type.

6. Contribution Flow

Contributions post immediately — there is no approval step — but every posted record can still be corrected through a controlled Void, never a silent delete.

7. Financial Management Flow

Annual Budgets and Disbursements are approved before money moves; Over-the-Counter (OTC) Payments taken at the counter post immediately since they are already backed by a receipt in hand.
